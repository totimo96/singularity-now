Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

In short

Singularity Now does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no connection to our own servers. The game works completely offline.

Data on your device

Your game progress, settings (music, language) and whether you bought the full version are stored only locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

In-app purchase

The full version purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive any payment or account details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not use cookies or analytics. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Since we do not store personal data, we usually hold no data about you. For questions, please write to the email address above.
